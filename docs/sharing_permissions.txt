Sharing and Permissions in Notion

Notion lets you share individual pages or your whole workspace with other people,
with fine-grained control over what each person is allowed to do.

Sharing a page
Open a page and click "Share" in the top-right corner. You can invite people by
email, or create a link to share. Permissions you grant on a page are inherited by
all of its sub-pages, so sharing a top-level page shares everything beneath it.

Permission levels
There are four access levels, from most to least powerful:
- Full access: can edit content and also share the page with others.
- Can edit: can edit content but cannot change sharing settings.
- Can comment: can read and leave comments, but cannot edit the content.
- Can view: can only read the page.
Grant the lowest level that still lets a person do their job; this is the principle
of least privilege.

Sharing to the web
Toggle "Share to web" to publish a page as a public website that anyone with the
link can view, no Notion account required. You can optionally allow public search
engine indexing, or allow visitors to duplicate the page into their own workspace.
Turn this off for anything confidential.

Teamspaces
In a Notion workspace, a teamspace groups pages and members for a department or
project. Members of a teamspace share access to its pages by default. A workspace
owner can set a teamspace to open (any member can join) or closed (membership is
managed by admins).

Guests vs members
A member belongs to your workspace and may have broad access. A guest is invited to
specific pages only and cannot see the rest of the workspace. Use guests for
external collaborators such as clients or contractors.
